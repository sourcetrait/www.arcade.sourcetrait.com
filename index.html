Orbit

In development ...

Orbit is a massively multiplayer dual-stick space shooter.

Large organized teams of pilots battle over resources that pass through their orbit. Each pilot flies an Orb of their own design on missions assigned by their chain-of-command.

Traits allow customization of flight characteristics, ship attributes, and equipment. The sky's the limit, but it'll cost you each time you phase in.

Fluid dual-stick motion is supported for gamepads, including the Steam Deck.

Dog fights are fast-paced. Scavenging for resources is strategic. Your team will need to be good at both to survive.
